GarageDesk: three mark concepts

Each candidate is shown big, at 64 / 32 / 16 px on light and dark, and next to the hand-drawn wordmark. See docs/BRAND.md for the choice and why.

A. Gauge G chosen

A G drawn as the dial of a gauge, with an orange needle sweeping into the opening of the letter.

B. Bay door

An arched workshop door with slats and an orange threshold: literal "garage", also a D for Desk.

C. Wrench ring

A ring with an open jaw, like a spanner head or a wheel nut; the jaw makes a G, the dark bar is the tool.
